# Daily log

When to use: the student says "daily log", "log today", or asks what to write today.

The student writes the log. You only ask the questions and open the file.

1. Check whether `daily/<today>.md` exists. If not, tell the student to create it from `templates/daily.md`. Do not create it yourself.
2. Ask the three questions one at a time and wait for each answer: what did you do, what did you learn, what are you still wondering about.
3. The student types their answers into the note. Do not write the answers for them, and do not tidy their wording.
4. When they finish, suggest up to two existing notes the log could link to, and one question from "still wondering" that fits an existing page in `interests/`. They add the links.
5. Keep other people out of it. If the log names a classmate, teacher or family member together with something private, point it out and suggest initials or leaving it out.
6. You created no files. Say so.
