# How LEDs affect plant growth

Sample source for trying the ingest skill. Written for this starter vault.

Plants do not use all colours of light equally. Chlorophyll absorbs red and blue light most strongly, so those colours drive most of photosynthesis. Green light is mostly reflected, which is why leaves look green, but some of it passes deeper into the leaf and is used there.

Indoor growers use this. Red light tends to encourage stem growth and flowering. Blue light tends to produce shorter, sturdier plants with thicker leaves. A mix of the two usually grows healthier plants than either alone.

Anyone comparing colours has to control intensity. A blue LED and a red LED with the same power rating do not deliver the same number of photons to the plant, so a fair test measures or matches the light reaching the leaves, not the wattage on the box.
