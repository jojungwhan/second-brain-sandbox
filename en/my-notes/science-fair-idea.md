# Science fair idea

Measure how light colour changes plant growth. Grow the same seedlings under red, blue, green and white LEDs.

- Ties together [[photosynthesis]] and [[light-and-colour]].
- Need: 4 LED strips, 12 seedlings, a ruler, 3 weeks.
- Open question: how do I keep brightness equal across colours?
