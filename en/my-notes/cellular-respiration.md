# Cellular respiration

The reverse direction of [[photosynthesis]]: glucose and oxygen in, carbon dioxide, water and ATP out.

- Glycolysis in the cytoplasm, then the Krebs cycle and the electron transport chain in the mitochondria.
- Most ATP comes from the electron transport chain.
