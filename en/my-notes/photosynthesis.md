# Photosynthesis

Plants make glucose from carbon dioxide and water using light.

- Happens in the chloroplast.
- Light reactions make ATP and NADPH and release oxygen.
- The Calvin cycle uses ATP and NADPH to fix carbon dioxide into sugar.

Still unsure: why are leaves green if red and blue light do most of the work? See [[light-and-colour]].
